# MoodTune asset credits and provenance

MoodTune includes demonstration audio tracks and cover artwork in `public/` so its built-in playback journey can be evaluated without relying on an external streaming service.

## Audio catalogue

All six demonstration tracks were downloaded from Pixabay and are used under the Pixabay Content Licence. Pixabay permits content to be used and adapted for commercial or non-commercial purposes, subject to the licence restrictions. Attribution is not required by the licence, but provenance is recorded here for academic transparency.

The files were renamed when they were incorporated into MoodTune. Five original download-page links and contributor names were not retained. This limitation is recorded explicitly rather than assigning unverified creators or links.
